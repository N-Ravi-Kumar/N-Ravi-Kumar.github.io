Rural Education Dropout Analysis

A data-driven investigation into student dropout patterns across rural regions. Survey data was collected, cleaned, and analysed to surface the socioeconomic factors behind students leaving education — with the aim of informing policy and community interventions.

Excel Data Cleaning Data Analysis Power BI (in progress)
Key Insights
1 Financial constraints and family responsibilities emerged as the leading cause of dropout among male students in rural areas.
2 Early marriage was identified as a significant and disproportionate barrier to continued education for female students.
3 Targeted financial aid programmes and community awareness campaigns were indicated as the most effective interventions to improve retention rates.
